Mark Motion Studies

Eight marks from the sheet, each moved by a different technique. Every loop starts and ends on the drawn mark, so a stopped frame still reads as the mark itself.

Click a mark to stop it at rest. Sheet R·C gives each mark's row and column on the source sheet.

Reduced motion is on, so marks play only while you hover, focus or tap them.
